css-art-rack

An illustrated 12U rack, rendered entirely in HTML and CSS. No images, no SVG, no JavaScript. Every blinking LED and spinning fan is a keyframe animation. Inspired by the Dell PowerEdge under my desk.

LEDs blink on @keyframes. The fan spins on a 2-second rotate loop. Disk activity indicators stagger so it actually looks like things are happening. One red blinking LED on the PDU because something is always failing somewhere.
