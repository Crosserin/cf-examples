solar-design-helper

String-sizing calculator for photovoltaic arrays. Enter module specs, inverter MPPT window, and design temperatures; get back NEC-compliant series/parallel combinations with cold-adjusted Voc and hot-adjusted Vmp. Built to prototype the math layer of a larger AutoCAD-generation pipeline from actual consulting work.

Not engineering stamped. This is a design-assist tool. Final permit drawings require a licensed PE, local AHJ review, and verified manufacturer datasheets. Don't electrocute yourself.
